# Sona — SLT Market-Research Survey

**Targeted survey · v0.1 · 10 Jun 2026**

A questionnaire for broad market discovery across the speech-and-language-therapy
(SLT / SLP) profession. The purpose is to **de-risk the single-design-partner fit**
documented in [`../strategy/sona-product-strategy-2026-05.md`](../strategy/sona-product-strategy-2026-05.md)
before we build further. The MVP and every persona were shaped by one interview
(Monal Gajjar — UK, paediatric, private). This survey is the instrument that tells
us how much of that shape generalises.

> **How to read this doc.** Each section opens with an italic _Research intent_ note
> for the team — **delete these before fielding the survey.** Question types are
> tagged `[single]`, `[multi]`, `[scale]`, `[number]`, `[short text]`, `[long text]`,
> `[rank]`. Branching logic is shown inline. Questions marked **★** are the
> "short-version" core (~6–8 min); the rest are the extended set (~12–15 min).

---

## Design principles for this survey

1. **Discover before validating.** Ask how they work *today* and where time goes
   before showing any solution concept. Problem questions first, solution
   questions last, so we don't lead respondents into agreeing with our wedge.
2. **Segment hard, then disaggregate.** Every headline result should be readable
   by care model (paediatric/adult), specialty, setting (solo/group/NHS/school),
   and jurisdiction. The whole point is to find the segments where our assumptions
   break — not to confirm the average.
3. **Don't anchor on Sona's vocabulary.** Avoid "intake / triage / carryover /
   parent" as the *only* framings — use neutral language and let the respondent's
   own terms surface (some say client/patient, appointment/session, caregiver).
4. **Measure willingness-to-pay against the status quo**, not in a vacuum.
5. **Recruit the next interviews.** The survey's second job is to find adult-voice,
   AAC, dysphagia, NHS, and non-UK practitioners willing to talk.

**Recommended channels.** ASLTIP member newsletter/forum, RCSLT special-interest
groups (SIGs), Bulletin classifieds, relevant Facebook groups (e.g. "Independent
SLTs UK", "SLTs / SLPs in private practice"), LinkedIn, and — for breadth beyond
the UK — ASHA Community, Speech Pathology Australia, and SAC channels. Target
**n ≥ 120** for usable cross-tabs, with deliberate over-sampling of adult and
non-paediatric specialties (which the product currently under-serves).

## Analysis plan (for the team)

Cross-tab **every** Section 2–7 result against the Section 1 segments. Specifically test:

| Hypothesis from the strategy memo | Question(s) that confirm/break it |
|---|---|
| Admin is a top-3 pain across all settings (not just solo-private) | 2.1–2.3, 2.6 × setting |
| The "new-client first-30-days" loop is a shared pain, not paediatric-only | 3.x × care model & specialty |
| The 4-outcome triage enum fits the market | 3.5 × setting & funding |
| "Parent summary" framing breaks for adult/school/care work | 3.7a, 6.4 × care model |
| AI distrust + "don't know what it can do" is widespread | 5.3–5.6 |
| £79/clinician/mo is defensible | 7.1a, 7.2, 7.3 × setting |
| Carryover (v0.2) demand generalises | 4.x × specialty |
| Where the product visibly breaks for non-paediatric SLTs | 6.3, 6.4a (qualitative coding) |

**Minimum segments to over-sample** before trusting any "the market wants X" claim:
adult voice, AAC, dysphagia, fluency, NHS-employed, schools-based, and at least one
non-UK jurisdiction. If a segment has n < 10, treat its results as interview leads,
not data.
