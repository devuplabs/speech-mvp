# Survey copy & recruitment posts

Ready-to-paste copy for the [market-research survey](market-survey-questionnaire.md).
Two registers throughout: a UK-default version and notes where to soften/swap for a
mixed UK/US/AU/CA audience. Replace `[Practice/Company name]`, `[contact email]`, and
`[link]` placeholders before sending. Keep the tone peer-to-peer, not salesy — these go
to clinicians who are tired of being marketed at.

---

## 1. Survey intro / welcome (top of the Google Form)

**Short screener — form description:**

> **A few minutes on how SLTs actually spend their time.**
> We're a small team researching the admin and workflow load on speech & language
> therapists — and what would genuinely help. This is research, not a sales pitch.
> It's anonymous, takes about 5 minutes, and asks **nothing** about your clients or any
> clinical data. There are no right answers — we want the real picture, including the
> messy bits. Thank you for the time. 💬

**Full survey — form description:**

> **How do speech & language therapists really work — and where does the time go?**
> We're researching the day-to-day admin and workflow of SLT/SLP practice so that what
> gets built next actually fits the job. This takes about 12–15 minutes. It's anonymous
> and asks **nothing** about your clients or any clinical/identifiable data — only about
> how *you* work. Honest answers, including frustrations, are the most useful thing you
> can give us. At the end you can opt in to a paid 30-minute follow-up if you'd like to
> say more. Thank you. 💬

_US/AU/CA swap: "SLT" → "SLP" / "speech pathologist", "GDPR" → "data privacy", drop
"ASLTIP/RCSLT" references in copy aimed outside the UK._

## 2. Consent line (Q0.2 helper text)

> Your answers are anonymous and used only in aggregate to guide product research. We
> don't ask for client data. If you later choose to leave an email for a follow-up chat,
> it's optional and stored separately from your responses, used only to arrange that
> conversation. You can stop at any time.

## 3. Screen-out / end messages

**Polite screen-out** (Student / Retired / Not practising / "No" on Q0.1 — send here via
*Go to section based on answer*):

> **Thank you — that's everything we need from this one.**
> This particular survey is aimed at clinicians currently in practice, so we won't take
> more of your time. We really appreciate you clicking through. If you'd still like to
> follow the work or share it with practising colleagues, drop us a line at
> [contact email]. 🙏

**Declined consent** (Q0.2 = No):

> **No problem at all — thanks for taking a look.**
> Nothing has been recorded. If you change your mind, the survey will still be here:
> [link].

**Completion thank-you** (confirmation message, Form settings → Presentation):

> **That's it — thank you, genuinely.**
> Responses like yours are what stop the wrong things getting built. If you opted in to a
> follow-up, we'll be in touch to arrange a time. Know a colleague — especially someone
> working with adults, AAC, voice, dysphagia, or in the NHS — who'd have a different take?
> Please pass this on: [link]. 💬

_Note: the over-sampling nudge in the thank-you is deliberate — it pulls in the segments
the product currently under-serves (see the analysis plan in the questionnaire)._

## 5. Small print to have ready (if asked)

- **Who's behind it / why:** "We're building tools to reduce admin for SLTs and want the
  research to lead the product, not the other way round."
- **Data handling:** anonymous; no special-category or client data collected; optional
  follow-up email stored separately and used only to arrange a paid interview; responses
  used in aggregate only. (Aligns with the privacy posture in the
  [MVP brief](../mvp-brief.md) and [strategy memo](../strategy/sona-product-strategy-2026-05.md).)
- **Incentive:** state it plainly if you offer one — e.g. "£25 voucher for completed
  30-min interviews" or "entry into a prize draw for the full survey". Response quality on
  the long version depends on it.
