## Candidate <n>: <name in ≤6 words>

- **One-liner:** what it is, for whom, paid by whom.
- **Problem & pain evidence:** what hair-on-fire problem this solves. Cite only
  admissible evidence (money already spent, budget lines, statutory duties,
  observed pull) — never "they said they would pay".
- **Buyer of record & budget line:** who signs, which existing budget it comes
  from, rough price point.
- **Distribution:** the channel, its gatekeeper, why the competitor/incumbents
  don't own it, and why 2 people can win it.
- **Moat (non-feature):** distribution / lock-in / data-eval / compliance /
  community / speed — pick and justify.
- **Asset leverage:** which of the 4 assets (eval harness, platform, compliance
  posture, domain depth) transfer, and how much survives.
- **Wedge → expansion:** first sellable slice, then the bigger thesis.
- **Riskiest assumption:** the one thing most likely to kill it.
- **Validation test (≤2 weeks, ≤£500):** design + success metric involving real
  money or committed behaviour. Include the kill criterion.
