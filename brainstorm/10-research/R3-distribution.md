# R3 — Distribution Map & Arbitrage

**To:** DevUp Labs founders | **From:** Claude research worker (GTM/distribution lens) | **Date:** 2026-08-14
**Premise accepted:** In 2026 AI SaaS, the product is a commodity within weeks; the channel is the company. Every pivot option must be scored on distribution first.

---

## TL;DR

- The channels you lost (RCSLT/ASLTIP visibility, UK SLT Facebook groups, Cliniko-adjacent scribe positioning) are **rentable channels that the competitor is currently renting, plus one defensible one (PMS integration)**. Don't refight them head-on; the incumbent playbook there is already run — Heidi alone is in [1 in 2 UK GP practices](https://webflow.heidihealth.com/uk), raised a [$65M Series B](https://www.aicerts.ai/news/heidis-global-care-partner-expansion/), and has a [Cliniko integration](https://www.heidihealth.com/en-us/blog/cliniko-integration); Magic Notes is in [200+ public-sector organisations](https://www.socialenterprise.org.uk/member-updates/public-backs-use-of-beams-ai-tool-in-social-care/).
- The **structurally underpriced channels in UK SLT in 2026 are demand-side, not clinician-side**: ~[75,000 children waiting, ~46-week average waits](https://thesendlist.co.uk/nhs-speech-language-therapy-waiting-times-uk/), EHCP plans averaging [42.6 weeks against a 20-week statutory limit](https://schoolsweek.co.uk/send-waiting-list-backlogs-at-risk-of-being-normalised/), tribunal hearings booked into [2028](https://www.changinglivessen.co.uk/blog/send-tribunal-appeals-in-england-a-parent-friendly-guide-to-ehcp-disputes-deadlines-and-evidence). Parents, SENCOs/TAs, and EHCP advocates are desperate, organised online, and ignored by every clinician-tool vendor.
- Rule for the pivot: **only pick a wedge where you can own the audience (email list, free tool, community, or embedded integration) within 6 months for <£5k**, and where the incumbent's clinician-channel dominance is irrelevant or an asset you can arbitrage (e.g., they generate notes; you generate demand and hand clinicians patients).

## 2. Defensibility Analysis

**Defensible once won:**
1. **Embedded integrations** (Cliniko write-back, school MIS, LA case-management). Workflow lock-in + data gravity. Highest defensibility available to you; note the scribe slot is taken — you need a different job-to-be-done in the same rails.
2. **Owned audience via free tool.** An email list of 10k UK SEND parents or 2k independent SLTs acquired through a free tool is an asset no competitor can buy from under you. This is the Upheal/OpenEvidence pattern (below).
3. **Community ownership.** Running the WhatsApp/Circle group for, say, "independent SLTs doing EHCP work" is defensible; *posting in* someone else's Facebook group is not. Admins hold the power — become the admin of a niche that doesn't have one yet.
4. **Content/SEO/benchmark credibility.** Programmatic SEO on queries incumbents won't touch ("EHCP Section F wording examples", "NHS SLT waiting time [town]" — note [MediMo](https://medimo.co.uk/nhs-waiting-times/staffordshire-and-stoke-on-trent/speech-language-therapy) and [TheSENDList](https://thesendlist.co.uk/nhs-speech-language-therapy-waiting-times-uk/) already do waiting-time pages — move fast). An open benchmark for AI-generated SLT clinical documents would make you the citation, not the advert.
5. **Multi-year public contracts** (defensible but not reachable directly at your size).

**Rentable (spend stops, channel vanishes):** association sponsorships, conference stands, podcast/influencer sponsorship, paid social, Google Ads, guest posts in FB groups. Use rented channels only to *fill owned assets* (list, community, free-tool users) — never as the strategy. The competitor "capturing" ASLTIP/community channels is mostly *renting* them; that position decays the quarter they stop paying and showing up. The only thing they may durably hold is a PMS integration slot.

## 3. Distribution Arbitrage Opportunities (2026)

1. **Parents on waiting lists — the biggest mispricing in the market.** ~75k children waiting, [46-week average waits](https://thesendlist.co.uk/nhs-speech-language-therapy-waiting-times-uk/), [21% SLT vacancy rate](https://www.mabletherapy.com/speech-and-language/why-waiting-lists-for-speech-and-language-therapy-services-are-getting-longer-and-what-you-can-do). Every vendor sells to clinicians; nobody arms the queue. A free "while-you-wait" home-practice/triage tool acquires parents at near-zero CAC and becomes a *referral engine you can sell to independent SLTs* — flipping the competitor's channel capture: they own clinicians' notes; you own clinicians' **next patient**.
2. **EHCP advocates and tribunal solicitors.** Appeals booked to [2028](https://www.changinglivessen.co.uk/blog/send-tribunal-appeals-in-england-a-parent-friendly-guide-to-ehcp-disputes-deadlines-and-evidence); LAs issuing ["rushed, boiler-plate" AI-drafted EHCPs](https://lancashire.thelead.uk/p/lancashire-county-council-send-assessment-ehcps-parents-unhappy-ai-tool-reports). Tools that draft parental representations, check EHCP Section F provision-specificity, or accelerate private SLT reports for tribunal have zero incumbent competition and a solicitor referral channel that markets itself.
3. **SENCOs and teaching assistants.** [Elklan has trained 100,000+ practitioners](https://www.elklan.co.uk/) — proof that the school-side paraprofessional market is large, organised, and buys SLT-adjacent products. A co-branded AI companion to an existing CPD provider inherits their distribution overnight.
4. **NHS band-6s moonlighting into independent practice.** The 21% vacancy/burnout dynamic is pushing clinicians toward part-time private work; they have no admin infrastructure and aren't yet in ASLTIP's orbit. "Private-practice-in-a-box starter" content + free tooling catches them *before* the competitor's channels do. Pair with universities/NQPs: free student tier costs you nothing and poisons the well for incumbents in 3 years.
5. **AI-agent app stores.** [ChatGPT app directory opened public submissions Dec 2025](https://www.datastudios.org/post/chatgpt-app-directory-and-gpt-store-marketplace-launch-sdk-features-and-platform-evolution), built on MCP; Claude's connector directory likewise. No credible SLT/SEND app holds the naming real estate ("@ehcp", "@speechtherapy"). Cheap lottery ticket with real upside; platform risk means it's a channel, not a home.
6. **Benchmark/open-source-led credibility.** RCSLT has published [AI principles](https://www.rcslt.org/slt-voices/shaping-the-future-in-a-digital-age/) but no evaluation exists for AI-generated SLT documentation. Publish an open eval set + scoreboard; every scribe vendor gets measured on *your* yardstick, and RCSLT/press cite you. This is how a 2-person team out-credentials a $65M-funded scribe.

## 4. Case Studies (2023–2026)

1. **OpenEvidence — free tool + verification moat.** Free, ad-supported clinical answers for verified physicians: [~65% of US doctors, ~$150M annualised revenue, $70-1,000 CPMs](https://sacra.com/c/openevidence/), [$12B valuation](https://medcitynews.com/2026/02/openevidence-healthcare-valuation/), grown [direct-to-clinician with no enterprise sales](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/openevidence-clinches-250m-series-d-rapidly-growing-its-reach-doctors). Lesson: in clinical niches, *free + credible + owned audience* beats sold software.
2. **Heidi Health — freemium land-grab into a rented-then-embedded position.** Generous free tier, then blanket free access for UK GP practices → [used by 1 in 2 UK GPs, 1.5M NHS appointments/month](https://webflow.heidihealth.com/uk), [$65M Series B](https://www.aicerts.ai/news/heidis-global-care-partner-expansion/), plus [PMS integrations](https://www.heidihealth.com/en-us/blog/cliniko-integration). Lesson: free tier is a distribution weapon, and integrations convert rented attention into lock-in. This is likely your competitor's playbook — study it, don't copy it late.
3. **Freed — community/word-of-mouth in the ignored SMB segment.** [$30M Series A (Sequoia)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250305397400/en/Freed-Secures-30M-Series-A-Led-by-Sequoia-Capital-to-Free-Clinicians-from-Administrative-Burdens-with-AI-Assistant), [$20M+ ARR, 17k-26k paying clinicians](https://venturebeat.com/ai/freed-says-20000-clinicians-are-using-its-medical-ai-transcription-scribe-but-competition-is-rising-fast) by deliberately targeting solo/small practices the enterprise vendors ignored. Lesson: segment arbitrage is distribution arbitrage.
4. **Magic Notes (Beam) — evaluation-led public-sector wedge.** A social enterprise rode independent evaluations ([48% admin-time reduction](https://healthinnovation-kss.com/ai-scribe-reduces-admin-time-by-almost-50-in-adult-social-care-assessments-independent-evaluation/), £23 saved per £1) and LGA case studies into [200+ organisations](https://www.socialenterprise.org.uk/member-updates/public-backs-use-of-beams-ai-tool-in-social-care/) and six-figure council contracts. Lesson: in UK public-adjacent markets, *published evidence is the channel*.
5. **Upheal — free-forever wedge in a prosumer therapy niche.** [Unlimited free AI notes + free HIPAA telehealth](https://www.upheal.io/pricing) as pure acquisition, upselling into a full therapist EHR. Bonus prosumer pattern: SLP creators like [Speech Time Fun (TpT → SLP Elevate)](https://podcast.feedspot.com/slp_podcasts/) and [SLP Now](https://slpnow.com/podcasts/) built audiences with $5 materials, then sold platforms — audience first, product second.

## 5. Implications — Distribution Rules the Pivot Must Satisfy

Any pivot option DevUp Labs considers must pass **all** of these; kill it if it fails two.

1. **Own, don't rent, within 6 months.** The pivot must build an owned asset (free tool with accounts, email list, admin-run community, or embedded integration) reachable for <£5k. If the GTM slide says "sponsor ASLTIP conference," it's the competitor's game — bin it.
2. **Enter through an empty channel.** First 1,000 users must come from a channel with near-zero incumbent saturation per the map: SEND parents, EHCP advocates/solicitors, SENCOs/TAs, students/NQPs, moonlighting band-6s, or agent app stores — not SLT Facebook groups or association newsletters.
3. **Buyer must be able to pay with a card.** No DPIA, no DTAC, no procurement committee in the first revenue loop. Parents, solo SLTs, solicitors, single schools: yes. ICBs, LAs, MATs at scale: later, via [G-Cloud/Spark listings](https://6b.health/insight/navigating-nhs-procurement-frameworks-a-guide-for-digital-health-innovators/) as cheap optionality only.
4. **The competitor's channel capture should be irrelevant — or an asset.** Best pivots make clinician-side incumbents *complementary* (you generate patient demand or evidence; their scribe writes it up). If your pivot competes for the Cliniko scribe slot or the FB-group share of voice, you've re-entered the fight you just lost.
5. **Free tier is the marketing budget.** Per OpenEvidence/Heidi/Upheal: at 2 people you cannot outspend anyone, but marginal cost of a free AI tool is near zero. Every rented touch (podcast spot, group post) must funnel into the free tool, not a pricing page.
6. **Publish evidence as a channel.** One independent evaluation or open benchmark (the Magic Notes/Beam pattern) is worth more in UK clinical markets than a year of ads — and it's the one credibility asset a funded competitor can't quickly copy without submitting to your yardstick.
7. **Reserve the agent-platform real estate now.** Ship an MCP/[ChatGPT Apps SDK](https://openai.com/index/introducing-apps-in-chatgpt/) presence under the category-defining name this quarter; cost is days of dev time, and the naming/ranking advantage compounds while the directory is young.

**Net recommendation on where the map points:** the highest-leverage pivot vector is **demand-side — a free parent/SENCO-facing "waiting list & EHCP" tool that becomes a referral and reporting engine sold to independent SLTs and tribunal advocates.** It is the only quadrant that is simultaneously unsaturated, ownable, card-payable, and *strengthened* by the competitor's clinician-channel win.
