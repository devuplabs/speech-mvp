# R1 — Competitor & Whitespace Landscape

**Prepared for:** DevUp Labs (Sona) pivot decision · **Date:** 2026-08-14 · **Author:** Claude research worker · **Analyst note:** All claims sourced 2025–2026 where possible; speculation is explicitly flagged.

---

## TL;DR

- **Generic documentation is over as a wedge.** Ambient scribing is now a bundled feature of every practice-management system (PMS) an SLT already pays for — Jane App launched a UK AI Scribe at £15/practitioner/mo (Jul 2026), WriteUpp shipped an embedded scribe to its ~13k UK customer base, Zanda (ex-Power Diary) has BizzyAI, and splose raised AUD $46M explicitly to push AI + UK expansion ([Jane/UK](https://www.intelligenthealth.tech/2026/07/20/jane-app-launches-secure-ai-scribe-for-uk-private-healthcare-giving-clinicians-time-back/), [WriteUpp](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/300741/WriteUpp-Launches-AI-Medical-Scribe-Giving-Clinicians-5.5-Hours-Back-Every-Week), [splose](https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/splose-raises-aud-46m-in-series-a)).
- **The most plausible "mystery competitor" candidates are splose, Heidi, WriteUpp, Ambiki, and Magic Notes/Beam** — each has shipped roughly Sona's clinician-side loop (intake/notes/plan/report) AND owns a distribution channel Sona doesn't (PMS install base, template communities, ASHA partnership, or local-authority G-Cloud contracts).
- **Money in 2026 sits at the platform/enterprise layer, not per-seat SMB tools:** Abridge $300M Series E at $5.3B (Apr 2026), Ambience $243M Series C (~$1B), Nabla $70M Series C, Heidi $65M Series B at $465M + the largest NHS ambient-voice deal (70k clinicians, 15 trusts) ([Fierce/Abridge](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/ambient-ai-startup-abridge-scores-300m-series-e-backed-a16z-and-khosla), [Fierce/Ambience](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/health-tech/ambience-banks-243m-series-c-investors-continue-bet-big-ambient-ai), [Heidi NHS](https://www.healthcare-management.uk/heidi-secures-largest-nhs-ambient-voice-technology-deal)).
- **Genuine UK whitespace clusters around statutory/clinical-judgment workflows, not notes:** EHCP/SEND expert reports for SLTs (current AI entrants — Invision 360, Agilysis, Beam — serve local authorities, not therapists, and face active parent-community backlash on quality), NHS SLT waiting-list triage (no dedicated pilot found despite ~46-week average waits), dysphagia, adult/neuro SLT, and assessment scoring ([Local Government Lawyer](https://www.localgovernmentlawyer.co.uk/education-law/343-education-features/62414-artificial-intelligence-in-education-and-ehc-plans), [SEND List](https://thesendlist.co.uk/nhs-speech-language-therapy-waiting-times-uk/)).
- **Sona's most defensible asset is probably not the app — it's the eval harness.** 54 schema-validated personas across 18 specialties/8 jurisdictions + golden-case regression testing is exactly what no scribe vendor, PMS, or NHS buyer currently has; safety/QA evidence for clinical AI in allied health is an empty and increasingly mandated layer ([npj Digital Medicine on scribe risk](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01895-6)).

## Plausible candidates for "the competitor"

Ranked by fit with "shipped roughly our clinician-side MVP **and** captured distribution":

1. **splose** — *best fit for a UK private-SLT founder's nightmare.* It is an allied-health-native PMS with a dedicated speech-pathology product, native AI that turns dictation into "report-ready writing" and SOAP summaries, intake forms, and now AUD $46M (Feb 2026, Spectrum Equity) explicitly for **deeper AI + UK expansion** ([SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/splose-raises-46-million-series-a-ai-allied-health/)). A private SLT choosing splose gets Sona's loop *inside* the system that already runs her diary and invoicing. Channel capture: PMS switching costs + 20k practitioners + NDIS-honed therapy workflows transplanting to UK.
2. **Heidi Health** — *best fit if the "channel" is clinician mindshare.* Free tier, a template community with UK and Allied Health Professional sections (SLT session-plan and report templates are community-shareable), session-prep/"pre-charting" features, and now the **largest NHS ambient-voice deal (70k clinicians, 15 trusts)** ([Healthcare Management](https://www.healthcare-management.uk/heidi-secures-largest-nhs-ambient-voice-technology-deal)). If UK SLTs Sona pitched are saying "we already use something," Heidi is statistically the most likely name. It doesn't do parent intake/triage — but clinicians rarely buy that separately once notes are solved.
3. **WriteUpp** — *best fit for UK private-practice channel lock-up.* UK-based, GDPR-first, ~13k UK customers in exactly Sona's segment (private allied health incl. SLTs), and its AI Medical Scribe ships inside the PMS with zero new logins and inherited compliance paperwork ([Newsfile](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/300741/WriteUpp-Launches-AI-Medical-Scribe-Giving-Clinicians-5.5-Hours-Back-Every-Week)). ASLTIP members disproportionately run on WriteUpp/Cliniko/Jane-class systems (SPECULATION: directional, based on category prevalence, not a member survey).
4. **Ambiki** — *fit if the competitor is US-facing.* Tenalog AI generates SOAP notes, progress reports and articulation error analysis; full pediatric SLP EMR incl. intake, scheduling, home-practice, IEP integration; and it captured *the* US channel by becoming an **ASHA corporate partner (2025)** ([ASHA](https://www.asha.org/news/2025/ambiki-becomes-new-corporate-partner-of-asha/)). Weak in UK, so a fit only if the founders' discovery was US-market.
5. **Magic Notes / Beam** — *fit if the competitor is on the buyer side of UK children's services.* AI session-recording → structured statutory assessments, now explicitly marketed for **EHCP backlogs at £10/report**, used by ~two-thirds of LA social-work teams and expanding into health via G-Cloud ([Magic Notes](https://magicnotes.ai/), [G-Cloud](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/985901463553519)). It didn't build a parent-intake→session-plan loop, but it captured the public-sector report channel Sona might have wanted next.

**Honourable mention (SPECULATION):** Mable Therapy — its MELCS screener (2025) is intake/triage for UK schools attached to an existing school customer base; if it extends toward clinician tooling it converges on Sona from the service side.

## Crowded vs whitespace

**CROWDED (do not pivot into):**
- **Ambient documentation / SOAP notes for allied health, any geography.** Feature of every PMS; price anchored at £15–30/mo add-on or bundled. Heidi/Jane/WriteUpp/Zanda/splose/Carepatron all shipped 2025–26.
- **Pediatric private-practice SLT "co-pilot" (Sona's current wedge).** splose + Heidi + WriteUpp pincer, plus US-side Ambiki/SpryPT/TheraPlatform. Also small TAM, as Sona already knows (~£2.4M ceiling).
- **US school-SLP planning/caseload.** SLP Now, SLP Toolkit, slptools.ai, Streamline, plus influencer-channel saturation.
- **Virtual therapy delivery.** Expressable, Parallel, Mable — services businesses with clinical payroll; Noala's death shows the D2C/hybrid version is brutal ([Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/noala-speech-therapy-raise)).

**OPEN (evidence of demand, no entrenched product):**
- **SLT-authored EHCP / SEND-tribunal expert reports.** Existing AI (Beam, Invision 360, Agilysis) serves *local authorities* drafting plans, and faces documented quality/legality backlash from the parent side ([Special Needs Jungle](https://www.specialneedsjungle.com/risks-benefits-artificial-intelligence-power-ehcps/)); nobody found is building the *therapist's* evidence-grade Section F report with provenance, statutory-compliance checking, and audit trail. Private SLTs charge £400–800+ per tribunal report; willingness to pay per-report beats £79/mo seats. Sona's clinician-reviewed, jurisdiction-aware architecture maps directly.
- **NHS SLT waiting-list triage.** ~46-week average waits, 65–72k children queued, and the NHS is in a top-down AI-triage buying mood (NHS App triage rollout to all users by 2028; £10bn programme) — yet **no SLT-specific triage pilot surfaced in any 2025–26 search** ([NHS England](https://www.england.nhs.uk/2026/07/nhs-accelerates-artificial-intelligence-rollout-to-cut-waiting-times-and-improve-care-for-millions/), [SEND List](https://thesendlist.co.uk/nhs-speech-language-therapy-waiting-times-uk/)). Sona's parent-intake→triage front end is the rare half of its MVP the scribes *don't* have. Caveat: NHS sales cycles vs a 1–2 founder runway is the known killer.
- **Adult/neuro SLT and dysphagia.** Every SLT product found is pediatric-first; dysphagia (high-risk, protocol-heavy, NHS-dominant) has no dedicated AI workflow tool in view. High clinical-risk bar — which is a moat for a team with an eval harness.
- **Assessment scoring & analysis** (vs documentation). Ambiki's articulation error analysis is the only production instance found; standardized-assessment scoring, language-sample analysis and screening (cf. Mable's MELCS) remain thin.
- **Eval/benchmark & assurance layer for clinical AI in allied health.** Scribes are shipping faster than anyone can validate them; peer-reviewed work is now cataloguing "uncharted risks" ([npj Digital Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01895-6)), professional bodies (APTA Sep 2025, RACGP Oct 2025) are issuing advisories with no testing tooling behind them ([APTA](https://www.apta.org/article/2025/09/19/apta-practice-advisory-on-ai-enabled-ambient-scribe-technology-now-available)), and NHS AVT procurement needs evidence. Sona's 54-persona/18-specialty/8-jurisdiction synthetic corpus + golden-case regression harness is, as far as this research can find, **unique in allied health**. Buyers: scribe vendors needing specialty validation, NHS/ICB procurement, indemnity insurers, professional bodies. (SPECULATION: small market today, but zero competition and Sona already owns the asset.)

## Implications for pivot

1. **Kill the "clinician co-pilot for private pediatric SLT" wedge.** It is simultaneously crowded (splose/Heidi/WriteUpp/Jane), small (£2.4M ceiling), and channel-captured. The mystery competitor's identity almost doesn't matter — there are at least five of them.
2. **Do not compete on documentation.** Notes are a £15/mo PMS add-on now; any pivot must monetize a workflow the scribes structurally can't reach (statutory reports, triage decisions, assessment scoring, assurance).
3. **Strongest near-term pivot: SLT-side EHCP/SEND-tribunal report engine.** Per-report pricing (£30–100 against £130–240 human drafting costs and £400+ report fees), an angry underserved parent/therapist side of a market where LA-side AI is distrusted, and direct reuse of Sona's clinician-review + jurisdiction-aware stack. Sell via ASLTIP/independent-SLT networks *and* as an integration into WriteUpp/Cliniko marketplaces rather than against them.
4. **Second pivot lane: waiting-list triage as an NHS/ICB product**, riding the July 2026 NHS AI-acceleration mandate — but only if the founders can fund a 12–18-month public-sector sales cycle or find an incumbent (e.g., a PMS or Beam-like G-Cloud vendor) to OEM the intake→triage front end. Otherwise treat as partnership material, not a company.
5. **Monetize the eval harness now, regardless of pivot.** Offer specialty-validation-as-a-service to scribe/PMS vendors entering allied health (splose UK launch, Jane UK launch, Heidi NHS deployments all need exactly this) and to NHS AVT procurement. Zero marginal cost, no channel conflict, converts competitors into customers.
6. **Adult SLT/dysphagia is defensible whitespace but slower:** clinical-risk barriers suit Sona's eval posture, yet the buyer is mostly NHS — pair it with lane 4, not standalone.
7. **Distribution lesson from the losers and winners:** Noala died channel-less; Ambiki bought ASHA; Beam owns G-Cloud; splose owns the PMS. Whatever Sona picks, the channel decision (professional body partnership, PMS marketplace, or public procurement) should be made *before* the product build, not after.
8. **Speed matters on lane 3:** Beam is one templating decision away from an SLT-facing EHCP product, and Heidi's community could crowdsource one. The window is real but likely 12–18 months (SPECULATION).
